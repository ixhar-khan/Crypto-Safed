State Bank of Pakistan (SBP) Advisory on Virtual Assets

The State Bank of Pakistan has repeatedly cautioned the public that cryptocurrencies and virtual assets are not recognized as legal tender in Pakistan. SBP has not authorized any bank, payment system operator, or financial institution to process, hold, or transfer digital currencies or virtual tokens on behalf of customers.

Individuals dealing in virtual assets do so entirely at their own risk. There is no regulatory protection, deposit insurance, or recourse through SBP for losses arising from crypto investments, exchange failures, or fraudulent schemes. SBP has directed all regulated financial institutions to refrain from facilitating transactions related to virtual currencies, including crypto exchanges and token sales.

The public is advised to remain cautious of platforms promising guaranteed or fixed high returns on crypto deposits, as such schemes are commonly associated with fraud and are not sanctioned by any Pakistani financial regulator.
