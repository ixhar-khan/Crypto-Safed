Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP) Investor Warning on Virtual Assets and Token Offerings

The Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan has issued repeated public warnings regarding investment schemes involving cryptocurrencies, tokens, and so-called virtual asset offerings. SECP has clarified that virtual assets, including tokens issued through Initial Coin Offerings (ICOs) or airdrops, are not registered or regulated as securities under Pakistani law unless specifically approved.

Investors should be aware that companies or individuals soliciting investment in crypto projects, particularly those promising fixed or unusually high returns, guaranteed profits, or multi-level referral bonuses, may be operating unauthorized investment schemes. SECP has taken enforcement action against several such schemes in the past and continues to monitor public complaints.

Before participating in any token sale, airdrop, or crypto investment opportunity, individuals are encouraged to verify the legitimacy of the issuing entity, check for any SECP registration or license, and avoid schemes lacking transparent team information, audited smart contracts, or verifiable business operations.
